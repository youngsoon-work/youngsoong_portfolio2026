SecureAge — Digital Campaign & Brand System

Client
SecureAge Technology
Role
Creative Designer
Year
2022

About the project

A multi-channel digital advertising campaign for SecureAge, a cybersecurity company. Running primarily across the Google Display Network alongside paid social (LinkedIn, Twitter, Reddit), the campaign translated the company’s brand values into a consistent visual language, spanning corporate brand awareness and product promotion, localized for EN and JP markets.

My role

I created SecureAge’s photography guideline, translating the company’s brand values into a company-wide visual system, shared with internal teams and external agencies to keep every touchpoint consistent. For this campaign, I developed the concepts by matching copy to imagery and designed the master ad formats, which scaled into 150+ assets across formats and retargeting, produced with an external agency.

The brand’s values

Every image has to reflect the brand. Before any campaign work, I started from SecureAge’s existing brand foundation, its messaging and core values, and translated it into a visual language: SecureAge's photography guideline, a company-wide system shared with internal teams and agencies.

The message was to remove the human burden of data security. The personality was a “Serious Rebel”, serious about security but not typical. Three core values set the visual rules:

Honest

Real, not staged, not exaggerated.

Simple

Clear, focused, no complexity.

Non-intrusive

Natural, not aggressive, no overlay graphics.

Together these pointed away from the usual cybersecurity cliches like locks, code, and hooded hackers, toward something calmer and more natural.

From values to images

Those values shaped every image I chose. Cybersecurity is abstract, so I looked for natural metaphors that could make it felt, each image carrying a specific idea about what security means.

  1. Snow peak — “Data protection: every file, every place, every time”

    A mountain is always there. Protection that stays constant, present at every altitude. Comprehensive.

  2. Tree roots — “Data: a business’s lifeblood”

    What holds a business up isn’t what you see. The tree stands above, but the roots secure everything beneath it, like data. Honest, Real.

  3. Underwater (JP) — “データ保護を実現 / Achieving data protection”

    The depths are your digital environment; the sunlight is protection. Under that light, the fish, your data, move freely and safely. Non-intrusive, Free.

  4. Child’s hand — “Financial data compliance for tomorrow”

    “Tomorrow” isn’t just the future, it’s who we protect data for. A child’s hand held in an adult’s makes the abstract concrete: the next generation. Inspiring.

  5. Dandelion — “Let go of your data security worries with true file-level encryption”

    Letting go, in more ways than one. Worries released, and data set free to move. Free, Not aggressive.

  6. Tunnel — “Is your future fully protected?”

    The speed of security, moving at the pace of your growth. Confident.

Campaign at scale

One system, adapted across channels, formats, and markets. The master concepts extended into 150+ assets, resized for each placement, localized for EN and JP, and adapted for retargeting.
